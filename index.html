HRM1 Production

From Mill Docs, the internal rebar mill reference

The Rebar Mill Revamping Project is a focused upgrade of the existing bar mill aimed at significantly improving productivity and operating capability. The revamp is being carried out with technology and major equipment supplied by Wuxi Bolong Machinery Co., Ltd., covering key process areas from billet charging to finished bar handling.

Through this project, the mill's annual capacity will be increased from 300,000 TPA to 600,000 TPA, with an hourly production rate of 90–100 t/h, while expanding billet length from 6 m to 12 m and maintaining a product range of Ø10–40 mm rebars.
